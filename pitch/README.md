# Crew Air: pitch

Live deck: https://claude.ai/artifact/Kxr2SHzzCTs2JyCXoSFSsQ
Sources: `deck.json` (order) and `slides/*.html` (one slide each, speaker notes in `<aside>`).

1. **Crew Air.** Smoke and heat forecasts turned into a shift plan, so the site keeps working and stays within the law.
2. **Smoke days cost money. Air maps don't save any.** Idle crews, late projects, claims and fines.
3. **The rules already exist.** AQI 151 → employer must provide N95s (Cal/OSHA §5141.1). 95°F → high-heat procedures (§3395).
4. **The outcome we sell: the site keeps working.** Heavy work moved before the smoke, N95s on site before 151, building on recirculation before the plume.
5. **Address in, shift plan out.** Site + shift + crew → tomorrow's actions, logged for the inspector.
6. **No sensors of our own needed.** NOAA HRRR-Smoke, AirNow + PurpleAir, NWS heat, NASA FIRMS → rulebook → plan + log.
7. **One engine, many rulebooks.** Construction (start), farms, schools, youth sports, delivery fleets, outdoor events.
8. **Buildings: act before the smoke arrives.** A BMS reacts to its indoor sensor; we give it a forecast to recirculate two hours early.
9. **Who does what today.** Comparison of AirNow, PurpleAir/IQAir, Watch Duty, HRRR-Smoke, Carrier/ALC BMS add-on, OSHA Heat app, Crew Air.
10. **What we heard tonight.** Weather app, sparse sensors, why pay, hard market: our answers.
11. **Why me.** Built Air Signal (airsignal.app), a live air map; that's how I know sensors alone don't cover a job site.
12. **Join the team.** Contacts + LinkedIn QR.

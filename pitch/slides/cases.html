The engine knows no law; each rulebook is a data file. We start with farms, because heat is enforced every summer and smoke hits them hardest in fire years. Construction uses the same two rules. Warehouses got an indoor heat rule in 2024, which is also the building-systems angle. Schools and youth sports need a go or cancel call. And Oregon, Washington, Spain or Italy are each a new file, not new code.

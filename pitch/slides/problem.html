Here is the gap. We computed it from public data: EPA daily air quality by county times farm employment. In the 2020 and 2021 fire seasons, California farmworkers spent at least four and a half million worker-days in air at AQI 151 or worse, the level where the smoke rule kicks in. Over the same period Cal/OSHA issued eleven citations under that rule. One per four hundred thousand. And in a survey of three hundred Central Valley farmworkers, sixty percent got no N95 from their employer. The rule exists. Inspection waits for complaints, and workers are afraid to complain.

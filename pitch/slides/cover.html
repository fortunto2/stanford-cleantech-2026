Crew Air

A reading at the work site when the nearest monitor is 48 km away: the record a utility crew goes in with after a fire, and the same hours for buildings and the grid.

Rustam Salavatov · Asher Popper · live at airsignal.app/crew

Hi, I'm Rustam, this is Asher. Crew Air turns smoke and heat forecasts into the shift plan California law already requires from every outdoor employer, farm or utility, checks that plan against satellites, air monitors and the crew itself, and when the nearest monitor is fifty kilometres away, sends for a reading. It's live, you can open it now.

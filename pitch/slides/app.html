The same thing in the crew's pocket. The plan, big and simple: one number, one action. The sky view pins the satellite fire detections and the nearest monitor on the real horizon through the camera, so "is that smoke coming to us" has an answer you can see. And the worker can point the phone at the sky: a vision model answers "is there haze, is the ridge visible", with a confidence, and that photo becomes a report with evidence. Privacy is built in: faces are blurred and the sky is cropped on the phone, only the sky leaves the device.

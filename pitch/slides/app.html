The same thing in the crew's pocket, a native iPhone app in English and Spanish. The plan: one number, one action, and when a satellite sees a fire nearby, the sensor task with its bearing and the record of how the air was determined. Check the sky: point the camera at the horizon, a vision model answers whether there is smoke, and here it says the sky looks worse than the forecast, so the worker can report it in one tap. The report goes to the crew lead, who sees a countdown and the evidence and marks it fixed. We checked the vision model before trusting it: twelve photos with known answers, right eleven or twelve times out of twelve and never wrong; when it's unsure, it asks for another photo. Faces are blacked out on the phone before anything leaves it.

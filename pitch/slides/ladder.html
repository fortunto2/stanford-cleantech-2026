Enforcement waits for complaints, and workers don't complain because they're afraid. So the product gives them a safe first step. A worker taps "smoke" or "no N95s here", no name, and the report goes to the crew lead with the evidence for that hour attached. The law already requires employers to encourage exactly these reports. If nobody acts within thirty minutes, the page drafts a Cal/OSHA complaint with the same evidence and the right agency for the state, and the worker files it in one paste. We never file on their behalf. Unions and legal aid come next, once they agree to receive reports. That third step is what makes the first two work: fixing it in thirty minutes is always cheaper. I did this before: back home in Bashkortostan we got people to report bad air with evidence, and that's what finally made the agencies act.

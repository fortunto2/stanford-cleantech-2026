We pulled every Cal/OSHA violation since July 2019 from the Department of Labor's enforcement data. Heat gets enforced: about a thousand to twelve hundred violations every year, over eight million dollars in penalties. Smoke barely exists: nineteen violations in seven years, sixteen of them in 2021. In 2020, the year farmworkers spent three point three million days in smoke, zero. Inspectors cite what they can see on paper, the written heat plan. That is why we sell the heat paperwork and carry smoke on the same sheet, and why the worker report matters: smoke enforcement starts with a complaint.

# Tracks and shortlist

## Tracks (from the organizer guide)

**Track 1, Energy & the Grid** (judged by Silicon Valley Clean Energy)
1. Make time-of-use rates make sense: help people see the cost and carbon of their consumption and
   know the good and bad times to act. Physical or virtual product.
2. Open build: smarter home energy use, balancing supply and demand.

**Track 2, Climate & Health** (TotalEnergies, Stanford Sustainability Accelerator)
1. Heat action plan audit: an LLM pipeline that finds US state and metro heat/cold plans, codes their
   commitments, scores them against evidence of what works.
2. Dengue surveillance routing: predicted breeding sites → cost-effective inspection routes under
   staff, time and budget limits.

**Track 3, Resilient Systems** (SE Ventures)
1. AI agents for sustainable home automation (Apple's new Home strategy, Google, Tesla ecosystems):
   efficient, decarbonized, secure home.
2. Predictive outage and reliability mapping: outage records + weather + infrastructure → where
   outages hit hardest → recommend distributed energy resources (paper: ReliAdapt, Rajagopal,
   Majumdar & Cezar).

## Shortlist (ideation only, no code before Fri 17:00)

1. **Track 2.1 heat plan audit.** Closest to what Rustam does every day: structured LLM extraction
   with schemas (SGR), a scoring rubric, a citations table. Well specified, demoable in 3 minutes as
   a map or ranked table of states with "plan says X, evidence says Y". Solo-capable, but the rules
   need a team of 2+.
2. **Track 3.1 home agents.** Fits his personal-agent work and the home-server plan. Risk: judges
   from SE Ventures want hardware ecosystems; a demo without real devices looks thin.
3. **Track 1.1 TOU rates.** A clear consumer product; needs SVCE rate tables and a sample load
   profile. Strong if the team has a designer.

Questions to settle at the opening: team, track, which data the mentors already have prepared.

## Decision (Fri 23.10, 19:00)

Crew Air, Track 2 (Track 3 as the buildings/BMS variant). Reasons and feedback:
- consumer air maps are crowded (AirNow, PurpleAir, IQAir, Watch Duty); the gap is action for the
  person legally responsible (Cal/OSHA §5141.1, §3395), see the deck's comparison slide;
- Gregory (SE Ventures): monitoring alone failed after 2021, sell the outcome, follow the money,
  top line beats efficiency.
